## 📚 Learning Log

### Class 1 — Introduction to AI and Machine Learning

**What is AI?**
AI is the science of making machines perform tasks that normally require human intelligence such as visual perception, decision making and language translation.

**Why AI Now?**
We are in an AI boom driven by two things: the explosion of data worldwide and the massive growth in computing power capable of processing that data.

**AI vs ML vs Deep Learning**
AI is the overarching goal. Machine Learning is a specific subset of AI where algorithms learn patterns directly from data instead of being explicitly programmed. Deep Learning is a further subset of ML that uses complex, multi-layered neural networks.

**Applications in Real Life**
AI is transforming industries globally including agriculture, energy, healthcare, finance and transportation.

**AI Workflow**
Every AI project follows the same pipeline: Data → Model → Deployment.
